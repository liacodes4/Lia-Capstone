# Pediatric Cancer Research Analysis

## Project Description

This project looks at whether pediatric cancers that have a greater impact on children are receiving a similar amount of research attention as other pediatric cancers. I plan to compare cancer rates, survival rates, and death rates with the number of clinical trials and research articles for different types of pediatric cancer. I chose this topic because of my internship at the Pediatric Cancer Foundation of the Lehigh Valley. During my internship, I realized how meaningful this type of work was to me and became interested in using data to look at pediatric cancer research.

## Data Sources

I plan to use:

* National Cancer Institute SEER
* ClinicalTrials.gov
* My school's library database for research articles

## Project Structure

* `data/raw/` - Original datasets
* `data/processed/` - Cleaned and transformed datasets
* `notebooks/` - Jupyter notebooks
* `src/` - Python scripts
* `reports/` - Reports and visualizations
